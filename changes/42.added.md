PennyLane parameter broadcasting runs as one batched execution on `ketjet.qubit` with `backend="jax"`: an exact broadcast circuit is mapped with `jax.vmap` from one compilation, when executed and under `diff_method="backprop"`, instead of being split into one circuit per value. Results equal `default.qubit`'s. The NumPy backend, shots, adjoint and parameter-shift still split the circuit, as PennyLane's own devices do.
