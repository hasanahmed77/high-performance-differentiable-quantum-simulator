`python -m benchmarks.capacity` measures how many qubits the JAX backend fits on a device. On the RTX 4060 (8 GB) that is 25 qubits in complex64 and 24 in complex128, for a forward pass or an adjoint gradient, and 21 and 20 for backprop through a 4-layer ansatz. plan.md's capacity table now lists these measured numbers.
